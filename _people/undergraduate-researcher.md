NTU undergraduates interested in CFD, high-performance computing, machine learning for fluids, or aerospace design optimization can do research with the lab through URECA or a Final Year Project (FYP).
A URECA project can continue into your FYP, and later into a PhD or MEng by research in the lab.
See [Available Positions](/contact_us/#join-us) to get in touch.
