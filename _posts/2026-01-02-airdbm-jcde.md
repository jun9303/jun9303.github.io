Dr. Lee and H. M. Sheikh present AirDbM in the [Journal of Computational Design and Engineering](https://doi.org/10.1093/jcde/qwaf124), vol. 13, no. 1. From over 1600 UIUC airfoils, AirDbM picks 12 baselines that reconstruct 99% of the database with a mean absolute error below 0.005. Its Pareto front has a larger hypervolume than that of an earlier DbM study with more baselines. It also suits reinforcement-learning airfoil generation better than conventional parameterizations.
