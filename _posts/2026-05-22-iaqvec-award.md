A paper by J. Park, Dr. Lee, and colleagues received the Best Paper Award at IAQVEC 2026, the 12th International Conference on Indoor Air Quality, Ventilation & Energy Conservation in Buildings. The conference took place in Los Angeles, CA, from May 18 to 22. The study simulates condensation risk in radiant cooling panels with elevated air movement. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1051/e3sconf/202671602027).
