C. Duarte, P. Raftery, Dr. Lee, and A. S. Solmaz published "Room Surface Convective Heat Transfer with Ceiling Fans and Its Effect on Radiant Cooling Systems" in the [Journal of Building Performance Simulation](https://doi.org/10.1080/19401493.2026.2656928). OpenFOAM simulations give convective heat transfer coefficients for rooms with ceiling fans, which then feed EnergyPlus models of radiant cooling. Fans raised median cooling heat transfer rates by up to 47% and gave a median cooling effect of up to 4.8 K.
