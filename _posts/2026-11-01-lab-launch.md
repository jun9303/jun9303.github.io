Prof. Sangjoon Lee joins the [NTU School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/mae) as Assistant Professor in November 2026 and starts the Predictive Fluid and Aeroscience Lab. The lab works on computational and predictive science at the intersection of fluid mechanics and machine-intelligence systems. A conventional CFD run can take days of computation, so we aim to build lean, fast aerophysics prediction models that cut lead time in aerospace design. See our [Research](/research/) page.
