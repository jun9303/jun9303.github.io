Dr. Lee and P. S. Marcus published "Transient Growth of a Wake Vortex and its Initiation via Inertial Particles" in the [Journal of Fluid Mechanics](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2025.253), vol. 1014, A16. It shows that viscous critical-layer eigenmodes drive the optimal transient growth of a strong swirling q-vortex. In two-way coupled vortex-particle simulations inspired by ice crystals in contrails, particles placed at the edge of the vortex core start this growth.
