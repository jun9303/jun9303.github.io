Dr. Lee is Co-PI on a Precourt Institute for Energy Seed Grant (SMC-001) from the Sustainable Mobility Center at Stanford University. The $100,000 grant supports work on highly efficient thermal management in hydrogen-powered aviation from February 2026 to January 2027.
