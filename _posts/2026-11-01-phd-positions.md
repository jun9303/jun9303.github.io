We are recruiting the lab's founding group, targeting the August 2027 intake for PhD students. Current NTU MSc students and undergraduates can join the lab through dissertation, URECA, or FYP projects. See [Available positions](/contact_us/#join-us) for details of each position. If you are specifically interested in working in the lab, don't hesitate to submit the [lab interest form](/contact_us/#how-to-apply).
